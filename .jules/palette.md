## 2026-06-11 - [Improving CLI scannability with emojis]
**Learning:** Plain text logs in shell scripts can be hard to scan quickly, causing users to miss important errors and troubleshooting steps in the terminal output.
**Action:** When writing or modifying CLI messages, use visual hierarchy by prepending '❌ ' to error statements, '⚠️ ' to warnings, and '💡 ' to actionable resolution steps.
