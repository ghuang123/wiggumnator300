## 2024-05-24 - CLI Visual Hierarchy
**Learning:** In terminal-based CLIs without graphical UIs, applying a consistent emoji-based visual hierarchy (e.g., '✅', '⚠️', '💡', '❌') significantly improves the scannability of setup and error logs, acting as the primary UX affordance.
**Action:** Consistently apply emoji prefixes to CLI output statements to establish clear visual patterns for success, warnings, errors, and actionable next steps.
