## 2026-07-18 - Enhance CLI Scannability with Emojis
**Learning:** Adding consistent visual cues (emojis) to CLI outputs significantly improves Developer Experience (DX) and scannability, allowing users to quickly identify errors, warnings, successes, and actionable steps without reading full text blocks.
**Action:** Use standardized emojis (❌ for errors, ⚠️ for warnings, ✅ for successes, 💡 for actionable resolution steps) systematically across all CLI scripts to establish a clear visual hierarchy.
