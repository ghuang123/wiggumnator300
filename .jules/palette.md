## 2024-05-24 - CLI UX Scannability
**Learning:** In purely CLI-based applications, developer experience (DX) and UX are synonymous with terminal output scannability. Important information, especially errors and required actions, can easily get lost in walls of text.
**Action:** When working on CLI scripts, employ visual hierarchy by prepending recognizable emojis (like ❌ for errors, ⚠️ for warnings, 💡 for actionable hints, and ✅ for success) to terminal outputs to make critical information pop out.
