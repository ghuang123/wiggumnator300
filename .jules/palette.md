## 2024-06-19 - [Adding Visual Emojis to CLI Logs]
**Learning:** In text-heavy terminal outputs, plain text errors are easily missed by users. Adding clear visual emojis (like ❌ for errors, 💡 for hints, ⚠️ for warnings) dramatically improves scannability and error recognition in the CLI.
**Action:** Always prepend appropriate emojis to standard echo or log statements (especially errors) in bash scripts to enhance CLI Developer Experience (DX).
