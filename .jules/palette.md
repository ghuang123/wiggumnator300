## 2026-05-13 - Enhance CLI Error Message Visual Hierarchy
**Learning:** Terminal errors are often missed or blended into text blocks, and users don't distinguish the problem from the solution. Using icons (❌ for the error, 💡 for the actionable step) vastly improves the scannability and user experience of CLI tools.
**Action:** Always prepend error declarations with ❌ and actionable resolution steps with 💡 to create immediate visual hierarchy in CLI outputs.
